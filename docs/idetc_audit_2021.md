# IDETC 2021 Ideation Audit

Working notes for auditing `conferences/2021 IDETC/` against
`src/design_research_problems/_assets/ideation/catalog.toml`.

## Scope

- Goal: identify IDETC 2021 papers that expose reusable ideation prompts,
  task briefs, or participant-facing study materials not yet captured in the
  ideation catalog.
- Focus:
  - participant-facing engineering design prompts
  - appendix or method materials that print reusable briefs
  - studies that materially deepen prompt coverage rather than only discussing
    ideation behavior or search behavior in the abstract

## Confirmed 2021 additions

- `DETC2021-70668`
  - added `prompt_wind_turbine_blade_repurposing`
  - appendix prints a reusable wind-turbine-blade repurposing activity with
    explicit sustainability, scale, and safety instructions
- `DETC2021-70805`
  - added `prompt_wheelchair_dorm_room_work_desk`
  - upgraded `prompt_wheelchair_peach_picking` from reconstructed coverage to
    primary verbatim coverage via the reprinted full problem statement
- `DETC2021-71825`
  - added `prompt_household_waste_disposal_unit`
  - methods section states the exact household-waste design challenge used in
    the multi-modal search-platform study
