Statement
---------

This catalog entry approximates the fifth planar roof-truss formulation from the structural essay study: seven uniformly spaced fixed roof load points with no enforced symmetry.

The state begins with pinned and roller supports plus seven loaded roof joints. Designers can add interior joints at asymmetric candidate locations, then add and remove members to explore non-symmetric layouts.
