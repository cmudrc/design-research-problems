Statement
---------

Conceptualize, design, and select a portable barbecue.

The concept may incrementally or radically innovate an existing model and should address the needs to be:

- easy to clean
- resistant to rust
- safe
- easy to move and carry
- capable of homogeneous heat distribution on the grilling surface
