Statement

At the Royal Albert Hall, lengths of slack chain from electric chain hoists can occasionally spill from collection bags, posing possible risks to people below.

Generate new ideas to prevent or better manage this chain spill problem. The solution should improve or modify the existing system without defaulting to impractical rigid industrial bags.
