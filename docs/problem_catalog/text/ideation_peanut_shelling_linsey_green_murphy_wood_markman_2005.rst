Statement
---------

Design a device to quickly shell peanuts for use in places like Haiti and West African countries.

The current solution is to shell the peanuts by hand, and no electrical energy sources are available.

Customer needs described in the study include:

- Low cost.
- Easy to manufacture.
- Quickly shell a large quantity of peanuts.
- Remove the shell with minimal damage to the peanuts.
