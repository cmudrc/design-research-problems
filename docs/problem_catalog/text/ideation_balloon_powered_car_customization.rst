Statement
---------

Design a balloon-powered model car assembly in which the body and wheels are customized.

Requirements:

- use a balloon fixed to a rear cylindrical mount for propulsion
- customize the wheels from a provided template
- customize the body within a provided bounding box
- keep the body below 5 cm^3 of solid volume
- keep the body continuous, manifold, and attached to the two

provided press-fit joints

- plan for zero redesign iterations on the wheels and at most

one redesign iteration on the body
