Statement
---------

Frothed milk is a pourable, virtually liquid foam that tastes rich and sweet. It is an ingredient in many coffee beverages, especially espresso-based coffee drinks (Lattes, Cappuccinos, Mochas). Frothed milk is made by incorporating very small air bubbles throughout the entire body of the milk through some form of vigorous motion.

The goal of this project is to design and build a new innovative product that froths milk in a short amount of time.

Customer needs:

- Froths milk in a short amount of time.
- Minimal instruction needed to use.
