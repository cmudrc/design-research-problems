Statement
---------

Design Challenge: How might we supply electric energy for essential household appliances during emergency blackout situations for low-income people in Puerto Rico?

Design Directives:

- Product should be affordable.
- Product should work indoors.
- Product should operate quietly.
- Product should be weather-proof.
- Product should be easy to set up and use.
- Product should be portable.
- Product should work with access to four hours of sunlight.
