Statement
---------

.. code-block:: markdown

   # Outdoor Toy for Ages 5-8

   You are an employee of a toy company designing a new outdoor toy for
   children. According to the market report, the design needs to consider the
   following requirements:

   1. An outdoor toy for children aged 5-8 that is mechanically interactive.
   2. The price and quality of the toys are important to the customer; it
      should offer good value.
   3. It must be safe.
   4. It should keep the child occupied and interested.
   5. It must last about 2-3 years of use.
   6. It should be priced between $20-40.
