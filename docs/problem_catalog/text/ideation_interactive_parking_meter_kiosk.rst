Statement
---------

If you transform a pay and display parking meter into an interactive kiosk, what application or service useful in your everyday life should be available?

The proposal should:

- not affect the appearance of the housing
- be relevant to a larger community
- use one or more of the terminal's functionalities
- be useful now or in the very near future
