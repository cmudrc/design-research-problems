Statement
---------

You are tasked with designing a solution to hold three hollow tubes securely in place and parallel to each other. All tubes must be held 2 inches away from a fixed wall (measuring from the wall to the closest edge of the tubes). The tubes are 1 inch in diameter and 3 inches long.
